Why this shape
  • Each email answers the question she has at that point. First what it looks like on a person, then whatever's still unanswered.
  • One code, last email only. Browse is the lowest-intent flow, so the code waits until content has had its go.
  • Customers see more, not cheaper. A week of fits, then what's new.
Build notes · Klaviyo
  • Trigger Viewed Product. Exit on order or checkout started.
  • Skip anyone already in abandoned checkout.
  • Reentry: once every 7 days.
  • Smart sending on. UTMs on.
