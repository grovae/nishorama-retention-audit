The audit covers where the gaps are. This covers the work. Two stores, seven flows each, and a campaign calendar running from week one.

01 · The build

Three phases over six months.

Each phase is the set of flows that goes live in it. Groundwork happens first, in week one, and is not counted as a phase.

Groundwork

Week 1 · before anything goes live
  • Klaviyo configured on both stores
  • Deliverability — domain, authentication, warm-up
  • WhatsApp and SMS connected on the India account
  • Audit of what is already built in India, so we rebuild rather than duplicate

Phase 01 · Capture

Weeks 1–4

Build a list you own, and close the gap nearest the purchase.

  • Lead capture popup — first-order code. Email and phone in India, email in the US
  • Welcome series — both stores
  • Abandoned checkout — both stores
  • Abandoned cart — both stores

Phase 02 · Depth

Weeks 5–12

Cover the rest of the journey, and make the second order happen.

  • Browse abandonment — both stores
  • Collection abandonment — both stores
  • Add to cart — both stores
  • Post-purchase series — US first
  • Order confirmation and processing — WhatsApp-led in India

Phase 03 · Compounding

Months 4–6

Act on the behaviour we now have six months of data on.

  • Category cross-sell — inside the first 60 days, India first
  • Win-back — lapsed customers, both stores
  • RCS pilot — India
  • Direct mail — high-value segments, US
  • Testing — on the flows carrying the most volume
Running through all three phases
  • India — 3 to 4 campaigns a week, across email, SMS and WhatsApp
  • US — 1 to 2 campaigns a week, across email and SMS

Campaigns start in week one and do not wait for the flows. In apparel they carry more weight than automation does.
