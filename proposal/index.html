01 · The build

Three phases over six months.

Capture first, then depth, then the compounding work. The order matters more than the speed.

Phase 01 · Capture

Weeks 1–4
  • Lead capture popup with a first-order code, both stores. Email and phone in India, email in the US
  • Welcome series behind it, both stores
  • Abandoned checkout, then abandoned cart
  • Deliverability setup — domain, authentication, warm-up before volume
  • India account audit so we rebuild what exists rather than duplicate it

Phase 02 · Depth

Weeks 5–12
  • Browse and collection abandonment, both stores
  • Add to cart, both stores
  • Post-purchase series — US first
  • Order confirmation and processing, with WhatsApp carrying it in India
  • WhatsApp and SMS fully integrated on the India account

Phase 03 · Compounding

Months 4–6
  • Category cross-sell inside the first 60 days, India first — where the repeat behaviour actually sits
  • Segmentation split between prospects, first-time buyers and repeat customers
  • RCS pilot, India
  • Direct mail to high-value segments, US
  • Testing on the flows that carry the most volume
Running through all three phases
  • India — 3 to 4 campaigns a week, across email, SMS and WhatsApp
  • US — 1 to 2 campaigns a week, across email and SMS

Campaigns start in week one and do not wait for the flows. In apparel they carry more weight than automation does.
