The VVIP list

Built over two weeks, rewarded with three hours

At Diwali the waitlist bought at 13.7% and spent 16% more per order than everyone else. Same idea here, with one clear perk.

Who is on it
  • Everyone who signs up on the early-access popup.
  • Subscribers who tap "Put me on the list" in one of three invite emails or two SMS.
What they get
  • Black Friday opens for them Mon 23 Nov at 9 PM ET, three hours early.
  • First shot at sizes before the site traffic hits.
  • Their own countdown email and SMS that night.
Deals

Two before the sale, four during it

Fixed dollar amounts, never another percentage, so margin holds on top of the sale. Each deal is an email link that applies it at checkout. Nothing to type.

Who gets what
  • Email goes to people who opened or clicked in the last 90 days. On launch, VVIP open, Black Friday, Cyber Monday and the last day, 180 days.
  • SMS goes to all SMS subscribers. It earned 10× email per person at Diwali.
  • People who ordered in the last 15 days are left out until 23 Nov. From 24 Nov, only the last 3 days, since the offer is bigger.
Running alongside
  • Welcome, checkout and browse flows carry the live offer and switch on 6 Nov and 24 Nov.
  • WhatsApp mirrors the deal days.
  • The House of Sundar list runs on its own page and its own sends, starting with 5,000.
