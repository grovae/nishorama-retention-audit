Why this shape
  • Prospects and customers never share a message. Prospects need a reason to trust a first order. Customers already trust it and just need the step removed.
  • One code, one place. NISHO10 appears only in P3. P1 and P2 recover orders at full price first.
  • Customers get no code. C2 uses the site's own automatic 10% over ₹2,599, which also lifts the basket.
  • Email answers the price objection. ₹1,700 vs the tailor's ₹899 is the #1 reason she stops at checkout (client persona decks).
Build notes · Klaviyo
  • Trigger Checkout Started. Filter: Placed Order zero times since starting this flow.
  • Split: Placed Order at least once in the last 3650 days → customers.
  • Smart sending off on P1/C1, on for the rest. UTMs on every message.
  • Every button links to {{ event.extra.checkout_url }}. The cart block is a dynamic table over the checkout line items.
  • C2's Liquid checks $value against ₹2,599. Confirm the automatic 10% is still live before build.
  • No SMS: Klaviyo SMS does not send to India. WhatsApp covers that role.
