Who gets what
  • Email goes to people who opened or clicked in the last 90 days. On launch, VVIP open, Black Friday, Cyber Monday and the last day, 180 days.
  • "Ends tonight" emails go only to people engaged in the last 30 days who haven't bought, so 18 days of sends don't wear out the list.
  • SMS goes to all SMS subscribers. It earned 10× email per person at Diwali.
  • People who ordered in the last 15 days are left out until 23 Nov. From 24 Nov, only the last 3 days.
Running alongside
  • Welcome, checkout and browse flows carry the live offer and switch on 6 Nov and 24 Nov.
  • WhatsApp mirrors the deal days.
  • The House of Sundar list runs on its own page and its own sends, starting with 5,000.
