Proposed plan · 7 Nov – 11 Dec · Global and India

Early access first. The biggest offer on the main days. A hard end.

Built on what Diwali taught us: the sale is won before it opens, deadline days beat discount days, threshold deals build bigger baskets than codes, and the list buys first. Global runs long and aggressive. India runs short, three weeks after Diwali.

Global · row.nishorama.com

Four phases, five weeks

Early access from 7 Nov as the team suggested. The offer steps up twice, then peaks on Black Friday to Cyber Monday, and a Christmas shipping deadline carries December.

Now – Thu 6 NovBuild the list

Early-access popup goes live

"Black Friday early access. Get the deal a week before everyone." Email and SMS fields. One teaser email and SMS to the engaged list. SMS earned 10× email per recipient at Diwali; this is where we grow it.

Fri 7 – Thu 19 NovEarly access · list only

30% off sitewide with code EARLY30

The site stays at full price. The code is the only way in, so it goes to the list, SMS subscribers and popup signups only. Free shipping threshold drops to $75 for the window.

Fri 20 – Wed 25 NovPublic pre-sale

30% off on site for everyone, plus a free gift above $150 for the list

The price drop goes public, no code. Early-access subscribers keep an edge: a free pair of linen pants above $150, the mechanic that built the biggest baskets at Diwali.

Thu 26 Nov 12:01 AM ET – Mon 30 Nov 11:59 PM ETBlack Friday to Cyber Monday · 5 days

Up to 50% off, plus $20 / $30 / $40 off above $150 / $200 / $250

Sitewide slabs (50 / 40 / 30) with spend tiers on top, no codes. A doorbuster drop each morning: five or six bestsellers at 50%, limited sizes. Hard end Monday midnight ET, no extension. Diwali showed Global halves the day after a deadline, so the deadline is the offer.

Tue 1 – Fri 11 DecChristmas deadline

25% off, and order by 11 Dec to get it by Christmas

Ten business days to delivery. At Diwali, "order today, wear it for Diwali" was Global's biggest day. Same lever, bigger occasion.

Sends
  • One email a day at most, engaged audiences only. All-list sends opened at 11% in October; engaged at 37–45%.
  • SMS on every phase open and close.
  • Sale versions of welcome, browse and checkout flows for the whole run.
  • Buyers suppressed for 15 days after ordering.
Why three steps, not five
  • Every extra step teaches people to wait for the next one.
  • 30% gets the list moving, the gift rewards loyalty without cutting price, 50% is the headline.
  • Codes barely got used on Global at Diwali (6 of 905 orders). EARLY30 works only because the site shows full price.
India · nishorama.com

Five days, one deal at a time

India did ₹2.99 Cr three weeks ago. Coming back at 50% off again trains the base to wait for sales, so the headline is up to 40% off, the daily deals carry the week, and the whole thing runs five days with early access the night before.

Fri 13 – Wed 25 NovBuild the list

Black Friday waitlist popup, two weeks out

Diwali's waitlist opened six days out and converted at 13.7%, spending 16% more than everyone else. Two weeks gives it time to fill. WhatsApp and email fields, with the one-hour-to-go promise.

Wed 25 Nov · 11 PM ISTEarly access

Waitlist and NishoCrew shop one hour early, on WhatsApp and push

At Diwali the waitlist was reached by email only, the weakest India channel. This time the one-hour-to-go goes on WhatsApp to the waitlist itself, with an app push at 11:30 PM and at 12:01 AM.

Thu 26 – Mon 30 NovBlack Friday to Cyber Monday · 5 days

Up to 40% off sitewide, plus one extra deal a day

Thu–Fri: spend more, save more (₹300 / ₹450 / ₹600 off above ₹2,999 / ₹3,999 / ₹4,999). Sat: free linen pants above ₹3,999. Sun: app-only extra 10% off (the app audience grew 62% in Diwali week). Mon, last day: free pants are back for 12 hours, and last sizes left. No personal codes; at Diwali they built the smallest baskets of the week.

Tue 1 DecHard stop

Prices go back up at midnight. No extension.

The Diwali extension paid, but a second one in six weeks costs trust. Announce the end date in every send.

Sends
  • WhatsApp: one broadcast a day per person, hard cap. Customers and verified subscribers only; prospects through journeys. Suppress anyone who has not read in 30 days before the sale, not during it.
  • App push: 3 a day, offset from WhatsApp, midnight launch push is the big one.
  • Email: engaged-only audiences until the Gmail fix lands.
  • Buyers suppressed for 15 days after ordering.
