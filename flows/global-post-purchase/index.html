Why this shape
  • Every email matches where the order is. Belonging when she pays, care when it arrives, a nudge to show it off once she's worn it, the next piece once she's sure.
  • No discounts. She has already paid full price or used her code. Post-purchase earns trust for the second order instead of training her to wait for one.
  • First-timers and crew split twice. At the start (belonging vs recognition) and at the end (second piece vs new drops).
Build notes · Klaviyo
  • Flow 1: Placed Order. Flow 2: Fulfilled Order.
  • Exclude cancelled and refunded orders.
  • D3 skips anyone who ordered again.
  • Smart sending off on F1/R1 and D1, on for D2/D3. UTMs on.
