05 · Email health

Building India's sender reputation step by step

Klaviyo is a new sender for both stores, so inboxes need time to trust it. Global is already there. India has a much bigger list, so we're widening it gradually.

Global
19% of people open campaigns and only 0.4% bounce. We can send to the full list.
Healthy
India
Engaged audiences open at 21–27%. Each send goes a step wider, but only after the last one cleared our bar: under 1% bounce and at least 15% opens.
Warming up
For the sale

India email goes to the ~47k most engaged subscribers. WhatsApp covers everyone else, so the full list hears about the sale without risking the inbox before Diwali.
