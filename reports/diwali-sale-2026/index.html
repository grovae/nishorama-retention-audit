Signups were mostly phone numbers, but the one-hour-to-go and launch messages reached them by email (15.6% open). The WhatsApp pre-launch sends went to the buyer lists instead. The best-converting pool was reached on the weakest channel.

Learnings

Six things for the next sale

01

The sale is won before it opens.

Day one was 32% of India revenue, 667 orders in the first hour. The two pre-launch WhatsApp sends out-earned everything that followed, the midnight app push alone did 53 orders, and waitlist members converted at 13.7% and spent 16% more than everyone else. Open the waitlist 10 days out, not 6, send the one-hour-to-go on WhatsApp to the waitlist itself, and put the best creative on the two days before launch.

02

Thresholds beat percentages.

Spend more, save more built a ₹4,214 basket and free pants ₹3,808. DIWALI15 and CREW12, the codes CRM sent, built ₹1,682. Run a gift or tier on the last day too. Global used codes on 6 of 905 orders; it did not need them.

03

Pause evergreen codes the night before.

APP10, NISHOKALISH5 and NISHO10 stayed live and stacked on 1,920 orders worth ₹38.4 L. An extra 5–10% off on 15% of orders, by default. Checklist item for the client's Shopify admin.

04

WhatsApp: verified numbers only, one a day.

Sends to customers returned 22.6×. Sends to prospects and unverified numbers returned 11.9× with half the messages failing; 241k never arrived. Customers got 11 broadcasts in 8 days and the last one was read by 2.9%. Cap at one a day, suppress non-readers before the sale, prospects through journeys only.

05

India email is a deliverability problem.

168k emails, 15% opened, 2% of the sale. Engaged-only sends opened 41%; non-engaged 2%. SPF, DKIM and Postmaster are with the developer. Until then, engaged-only audiences on every India send, no exceptions on sale days.

06

Global wants four days and a deadline.

Global peaked on "order today, wear it for Diwali" and halved once the shipping deadline passed. Codes and the extension did nothing. Next time: four days built around a real delivery date, engaged-only sends, and grow the SMS list, which earned 10× email per recipient.

Method

Store revenue is Shopify. Channel revenue is each platform's own attribution: Bitespeed for WhatsApp, Appbrew for push, Klaviyo (5-day window, Placed Order) for email and SMS. Windows overlap, so channel numbers are not additive. Pre-sale sends (16–19 Oct) are included at their attributed value; the direct measure of pre-sale email clickers who bought in the sale (₹1.26 L) is not added to the totals. India window Fri 23 – Wed 28 Oct IST; Global Thu 22 2:31 PM – Wed 28 Oct ET. Costs are Bitespeed message costs only.
